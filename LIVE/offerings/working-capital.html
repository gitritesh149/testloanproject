Our team helps MSMEs, traders, manufacturers, service providers, and growing enterprises secure the right OD/CC solutions based on their business needs and banking eligibility.

Overdraft (OD) Facility

An Overdraft facility allows businesses to withdraw funds beyond the available account balance up to a sanctioned limit. Interest is charged only on the utilized amount, making it a flexible and cost-effective financing option for short-term working capital requirements.

Cash Credit (CC) Facility

A Cash Credit facility provides businesses with continuous access to working capital against stock and receivables. It helps maintain liquidity and supports uninterrupted business operations, especially for businesses dealing with regular inventory cycles.
