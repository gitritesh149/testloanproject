Turn Your Dream of Owning a Home Into Reality

At Capital Growth Advisory, we help individuals and families turn their dream of owning a home into reality with reliable and hassle-free Home Loan solutions. With our strong banking expertise and professional guidance, we assist clients in securing the right home loan options with competitive interest rates and smooth processing.

Whether you are purchasing a new home, resale property, under-construction property, plot purchase, home construction, or balance transfer, our team provides complete support throughout the loan journey. We understand that buying a home is one of the most important financial decisions in life. That is why we focus on offering personalized solutions based on your income profile, eligibility, and financial goals.

Our Home Loan Services Include

  • New Home Purchase Loan: Perfect for financing brand new residential properties and ready-to-move-in flats.
  • Balance Transfer Facility: Shift your existing higher-interest home loan to competitive, lower-rate bank ecosystems.
  • Home Construction Loan: Capital allocations structured specifically to fund the step-by-step building of your house.
  • Plot + Construction Loan: Combined financial assistance to purchase land and manage immediate architectural construction.
  • Loan Against Property Guidance: Expert advisory to unlock hidden equity from existing assets for your capital requirements.
  • Self-Employed & Salaried Profile Assistance: Custom-tailored files matching specialized underwriting criteria for both jobs and independent businesses.
  • Documentation & Eligibility Support: Step-by-step processing help to map your financial profile out securely.
  • Smooth Coordination with Banks & Financial Institutions: Utilizing our strong networks to accelerate verification loops and sanction tasks.
