Unlock the hidden equity values stored within your real estate holdings. Secure high-ticket liquidity transitions built for long-term corporate or individual targets without selling your structural assets.

Unlock the Financial Value of Your Asset

At Capital Growth Advisory, we provide reliable and customized Loan Against Property (LAP) solutions to help individuals and businesses unlock the value of their residential, commercial, or industrial properties for financial requirements.

A Loan Against Property is a secured loan where you can avail funds by mortgaging your property while continuing to retain ownership and usage of the property. It is an ideal financial solution for business expansion, working capital, education, medical emergencies, debt consolidation, property renovation, or other personal and business needs. With our strong banking expertise and professional guidance, we help clients secure the most suitable LAP solutions with competitive interest rates, higher loan eligibility, and smooth processing.

Our Loan Against Property Services Include

  • Loan Against Residential Property: Leverage flats, villas, or self-occupied structures to meet large-scale funding needs.
  • Loan Against Commercial Property: Unlock capital locked in owned office spaces, warehouses, or commercial retail outlets.
  • Business Expansion Funding: Gain long-term capital allocations structured perfectly to scale business operations and reach.
  • Working Capital Support: Secure dependable cash reserves to stabilize corporate operational cycles and cash liquidity.
  • Balance Transfer Assistance: Move your active higher-interest asset debt to low-rate banking ecosystems effortlessly.
  • Self-Employed & Salaried Profile Support: Tailored processing paths optimized for corporate firms, businessmen, and executives.
  • Documentation & Eligibility Guidance: Strategic management of your financial file to ensure baseline criteria are easily fulfilled.
  • Smooth Coordination with Banks & Financial Institutions: Speed up application cycles through our premium networks.
